Redactada para ocupar bien el espacio útil del dashboard con beneficios claros, escenarios reales y una promesa premium sin exageraciones falsas.

Prism Volume Booster is built for the everyday frustration millions of Chrome users know too well: videos that sound weak, streams that never reach a comfortable level, lessons that get buried under room noise, and calls or web players that force you to keep fighting with volume over and over again. Instead of offering a bare slider and calling it a day, Prism gives users a stronger, cleaner and more deliberate way to raise tab audio with real-time processing that feels premium from the very first click.

The extension is designed to stay friendly for non-technical users while still feeling powerful for demanding ones. Users can boost the current tab in seconds, keep site-specific gain preferences, choose automatic behavior for compatible pages, and fine-tune sound settings when they want more control. That means less friction, less guesswork and far less time jumping between weak websites and broken listening experiences just to hear content properly.

Prism Volume Booster also stands out by making its value obvious, not hidden. The product is transparent about how it works, why it needs permissions and what stays local on the device. Audio boosting happens in the browser, settings stay in browser storage, and optional observability builds are limited to sanitized technical telemetry instead of remote executable code. The result is a Chrome audio booster that feels serious, polished, trustworthy and built for people who want better sound without sacrificing clarity or control.

Whether the user is watching tutorials, following online classes, listening to long-form content, joining calls, streaming music or rescuing a quiet video from a frustrating website, Prism Volume Booster is meant to make the experience feel louder, clearer, calmer and more in control. It does one job, but it does that job with care: helping weak tab audio feel strong again.

Cada permiso va en su propio renglón para que se pueda leer rápido y sin perder tiempo.

  • activeTab: lets the extension work on the tab the user explicitly chooses to boost, instead of touching unrelated tabs in the background.
  • offscreen: keeps the manual audio engine alive in a hidden document so boosting can continue after capture starts and the popup closes.
  • scripting: injects the automatic booster runtime on compatible pages when the product needs tab-level logic to hook the active experience.
  • storage: saves local settings such as gain levels, sound controls, auto-boost mode and remembered site-specific preferences.
  • tabCapture: captures the current tab audio for the manual booster lane so Prism can raise and shape playback in real time.
  • tabs: reads the active tab identity and metadata needed to label sessions, target the correct tab and manage booster state cleanly.
